# Mural 0.3.0

Mural now teaches eight languages, listens better in each of them, and its voice works on Windows.

## Eight languages for Shadow and Dialog

Shadow and Dialog are no longer German-only. **Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese,
English, Norwegian (Bokmål) and Mandarin Chinese** join German, each with 1,500 Shadow phrases
from A1 to C1 and all 237 Dialog scenarios.

They are written for the country, not translated from German. In Spain you register on the
padrón and eat the menú del día. In Brazil you pay by Pix and take the coach from the rodoviária.
In Norway you sign in with BankID and deal with NAV. In China you book on 12306 and pay with
WeChat or Alipay. Where a German situation doesn't exist, it was replaced: Brazilian summers bring
a broken air conditioner instead of broken heating.

Chinese phrases and Dialog tasks show their pinyin underneath.

## Dialog: finer steps, fairer ticks

- **More steps, more scenarios.** Each scenario now has 12 (A1) to 25 (C1) tasks, up from four or
  five, so you can see your progress and the exchange has somewhere to go. Every level has at
  least 30 scenarios; there are 44 new ones at B1–C1.
- **Tasks done in any order now count.** Mural used to check tasks in list order only, so a step
  done early or late was missed, and saying goodbye could drop earlier ticks. It now checks the
  whole list each turn and reads your words as speech from a learner with an accent.
- **The Talk screen lists every task.** Done tasks are ticked and the next three outlined. Point at
  one to see its meaning. The list folds away behind its "n/total tasks" pill.

## Better listening with the Natural voice

Each language now listens with the speech recognizer that understood learners best when we tested
them, instead of one model guessing the language. That guessing is why Spanish could come out as
Portuguese, and Chinese or Norwegian in the wrong alphabet.

| Language | Listening model |
| --- | --- |
| English | Parakeet |
| Spanish, French, Italian | Parakeet, with Whisper as a second opinion |
| German | NVIDIA's German-only FastConformer |
| Portuguese | Whisper, told to expect Portuguese |
| Norwegian | Whisper, told to expect Norwegian, or NB-Whisper if you build it (see below) |
| Chinese | SenseVoice |

- **Short phrases are no longer lost.** With *Hold to speak*, a quick "Bom dia!" or "Come stai?"
  used to vanish. Mural now transcribes the whole hold when it heard no speech in it.
- **First words survive.** The start of an utterance was sometimes clipped ("Un attimo, per
  favore" became "Per favore").
- **French speaks again.** Mural's French voice had fallen silent.

### Optional: a better Norwegian listening model

Out of the box, Norwegian listens with Whisper, which caught about 43% of words in our tests.
The National Library of Norway's [NB-Whisper](https://huggingface.co/NbAiLab/nb-whisper-base)
(Apache-2.0) caught about 65%. Mural doesn't ship or download NB-Whisper. You can build it on your
own computer and Mural will use it.

**With the script (recommended).** You need Python 3.9–3.12, about 3 GB of free space, and a few
minutes. In the Mural source folder ([download the source](https://github.com/rustsky/mural-dioxus/releases/tag/v0.3.0)), run:

```
./scripts/convert-nb-whisper.sh
```

On Windows, run it from Git Bash. The script downloads NB-Whisper from the National Library's
page, converts it, and installs it in the right folder. See
[`scripts/convert-nb-whisper.sh`](https://github.com/rustsky/mural-dioxus/blob/v0.3.0/scripts/convert-nb-whisper.sh)
for what it does, step by step.

**By hand.** Convert [NbAiLab/nb-whisper-base](https://huggingface.co/NbAiLab/nb-whisper-base)
with sherpa-onnx's [Whisper export script](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/v1.13.8/scripts/whisper/export-onnx.py),
keep the int8 files, and put them in Mural's `voice-models` folder under this exact name:

```
voice-models/
  nb-whisper-base-int8/
    nb-base-encoder.int8.onnx
    nb-base-decoder.int8.onnx
    nb-base-tokens.txt
```

The `voice-models` folder is at:

- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Mural/voice-models/`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Mural\voice-models\`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/Mural/voice-models/`

Mural switches to NB-Whisper from the next Norwegian conversation. Remove the folder to go back.

The first conversation in each language downloads its voice models once. Depending on the
language, that is about 300 MB to 1.4 GB. To free the space taken by models from 0.2, use
*Delete downloaded voice models* in Settings. It removes all of them, and each language downloads
what it needs again on its next conversation.

## Fairer Shadow scores

- **Accents no longer cost you points.** "como estas" now matches "cómo estás", because whether the
  accent marks show up is the recognizer's spelling choice, not your pronunciation. Letters that
  are sounds of their own, like ñ and å, still count.
- **Numbers can be digits or words.** "5 minutos" now matches "cinco minutos".
- **Filler sounds are ignored**, such as the "嗯" a recognizer adds for silence.
- **Chinese is scored character by character**, instead of all or nothing.

## Windows

- **The microphone works.** Voice failed on Windows with a message telling you to open "Mural.app",
  and running as administrator didn't help. The cause was how the app page was loaded; it is fixed.
- **Microphone errors on Windows now point to Windows settings:** Settings → Privacy & security →
  Microphone → "Let desktop apps access your microphone".
